Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what personal information Skynex Solutions Limited collects through this website, why, who it is shared with, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Who We Are

Skynex Solutions Limited (“Skynex”, “we”, “us”) operates this website and decides how the personal information described below is used. That makes us the “data controller” under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (the “NDPA”). Our address is KS Pawa Plaza, Shuaibu Dagaci St., Suleja, Niger State, Nigeria.

For any privacy question or request, contact us at skynexsolutionslimited@gmail.com.

This policy covers this website. Our Trideta school management platform is a separate service with its own policy: Trideta Privacy Policy.

2. What Information We Collect and Why

We collect only what we need for each purpose below.

Contact form. When you send us an enquiry we collect your name, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message. Your company name and a budget range are optional. We use this only to read and reply to your enquiry and to keep a record of it. Our lawful bases are taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.

Email and WhatsApp. If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to send, such as your name, phone number and message, and we use it to reply to you.

Staff Portal. Skynex directors and staff can sign in to a private area of this website. For them we hold an email address, name, job title, role and an optional profile photo, and we keep their sign-in session on their device so they stay signed in. This is used only to run the portal and to publish content such as news and leadership profiles.

Public profiles and news. Leadership profiles and news articles shown on this website are published by Skynex. Profiles are shown with the knowledge of the people they describe.

Search. The search box on this website works inside your browser. What you type is not sent to us and is not stored.

Technical data. When you load a page, our hosting provider and the other providers listed below receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page. We do not run analytics or build visitor profiles from it.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use as few as possible. Here is exactly what is used:

  • Strictly necessary — your cookie choice. If you make a choice about cookies, we store that choice (yes or no, the date and a version number) in your browser’s local storage so we can remember it. Nothing else is stored.
  • Strictly necessary — Staff Portal sign-in. Only for staff who sign in. A sign-in session and a short summary of the signed-in person’s role are kept in the browser so the portal works. Ordinary visitors never get these.
  • Optional — advertising (Google AdSense). Our News pages can show advertising provided by Google. This may use cookies and similar identifiers and may process your IP address and browsing information. It is switched off unless you accept. If you reject, the Google advertising code is never loaded.

We do not use analytics tools, social media tracking pixels or any other optional technologies. The share buttons on news articles are ordinary links: nothing is loaded from a social network unless you click one.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with Cookie Settings in the footer of every page. Withdrawing is as easy as accepting, and it does not affect anything done before you withdrew. Google describes how it uses data in advertising at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Services We Use

We rely on the following providers. Some of them receive your IP address when your browser loads their resources, and one stores the information you send us.

  • Supabase — stores contact-form enquiries, staff accounts and published content. Our project is hosted in Ireland (EU West).
  • Google Firebase Hosting — delivers this website.
  • Google Fonts — website typography.
  • Cloudflare (cdnjs), Tailwind CSS CDN and jsDelivr — deliver our icon library, styling framework and code libraries.
  • Google AdSense — advertising, only if you accept (see Section 3).
  • WhatsApp — if you choose to message us there, that conversation is governed by WhatsApp’s own policy.

These providers act under their own privacy policies. We do not give them your enquiry details, other than storing them with Supabase as described.

5. Transfers Outside Nigeria

Several of these providers process information outside Nigeria. For example, enquiries are stored in Ireland, and Google and Cloudflare operate globally. We choose established providers and send only what is needed. Where the NDPA requires safeguards for such transfers, we will apply them.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. We share it only with the service providers above to run this website, or where the law, a court or a regulator requires it, or to protect our legal rights.

7. Security

The website uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Enquiries and staff data are held in a database with access rules that allow only authorised Skynex staff to read them, and staff accounts are limited to the tasks of their role. No system is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the regulator as the law requires.

8. How Long We Keep Information

We keep a contact-form enquiry until it has been dealt with and marked as resolved. After that, our staff may delete it at any time, and we do not keep it longer than we need it. We may keep it for longer only where the law requires. Staff account details are kept while the person works with us and are removed when their access ends. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you change or clear it.

9. Your Rights

Under the NDPA you have rights over your personal data. In summary, you can ask us to:

  • tell you whether we hold information about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it for a legal reason;
  • restrict our use of your information or object to it;
  • provide your information in a portable form, where that applies;
  • stop relying on your consent, at any time, where we use consent.

To make a request, email skynexsolutionslimited@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm who you are first. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible and within the time the law requires.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC): ndpc.gov.ng.

11. Children

This website is for a general business audience and is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us any personal information. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.
